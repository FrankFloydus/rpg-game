# Reference game systems research

Use this record when comparing Hearth Below inventory actions, encounter layout, progression, environment materials, or weather with the installed reference games. The installation evidence below is for design research. The project uses its own assets, tuning, and transactions.

## Reproduce the local checks

- **Path of Exile** uses the standalone Grinding Gear Games installation on this workstation. The Steam directories examined held logs. The standalone `PathOfExile.exe` and the executable retained in the earlier dungeon Ghidra project both had SHA-256 `EE1D81D36C2744FEE3B82A8305819D85BAD1F7C68B6BAC12300D32180FF0C059`. The retained `Content.ggpk` table sample carries patch label `3.30.0.4.3`. The executable hash verifies the replayed code. The table sample describes its archived snapshot.
- **Skyrim Special Edition** was Steam build `24914197`, executable version `1.7.104.0`, SHA-256 `846EFCCF0C1374D71F892907F46549560F2FCB0A75CB87A3EED438BAA0F1402F`. The installed base `Skyrim.esm` had SHA-256 `E198C3B85E5E48E0C92A6580D8F66E644256B68D812EE32B61735CF9B753DF73`. These samples describe the base game records.
- **Warhammer: Vermintide 2** was Steam build `25901688`. The inspected Lua was extracted from installed game scripts bundle `4781bad4b7d3a395` and decoded with strict LJD after verifying the LuaJIT header. The [Vermintide research workflow](vermintide-animation-research.md) covers the unpacker, header, and distinction between Lua, animation resources, and native analysis.

Ghidra 12.2 and Java 25 processed the matched PoE scratch project in read-only mode. The Skyrim session imported a byte-identical executable into a fresh scratch project. Target addresses came from PE exception tables and MSVC RTTI cross-references or vtables. `DecompInterface` returned bodies for all selected functions. In this workstation's scratch directory `%TEMP%/hearth-reference-expansion`, `ReferenceCodeBodies.java`, `pe-vtables.py`, and `*ghidra*stdout.log` retain the procedure, addresses, and completion results. `poe-native` and `skyrim-native` retain the decompiler results locally. Recheck addresses when the exe hash changes. A decompiler name or a raw table entry alone establishes only the code or data inspected.

## Path of Exile inventory actions

RTTI-associated callable tables connect cursor eligibility at `0x1402F9330` and `0x1402F9470`, stack at `0x1403135E0`, trade drop at `0x140325C50`, and a vendor-specific swap at `0x14054E020` to separate native branches. The cursor predicates take a target inventory-set and item identity and pass them to eligibility checks. Follow-up decompiles at the callees confirmed distinct routines for stack, trade drop, and the specialized Disenchanting swap. This establishes separate client actions. We did not recover the exact generic grid overlap policy, its highlight color, or authoritative server validation.

For Hearth Below, apply the same destination calculation for preview and placement. A valid swap fits the carried footprint within the destination and overlaps exactly one movable item. Tint the intersecting cells yellow and leave the rest of the footprint legible. On click, park the held item at the previewed anchor and carry the displaced item forward as a single transaction. Check standard placement, stack and use gestures, one vs. several occupants, failed bounds, cancellation, and that the item remains recoverable across pack and stash.

## Path of Exile room and encounter composition

The replayed dungeon callbacks at `0x142103F80`, `0x142102190`, `0x14205D600`, and `0x140BBCF00` are associated with room-layout generation, room-graph traversal, tile subobjects, and the client instance picker. The shipped asset index also includes multiple graph variants per named area. One installed Act 2 dungeon folder had ten graph entries. The pack archive has separate pack compositions, counts, formation records, level-related spawner groups, and strongboxes. The client's layouts and content records do not expose a complete authoritative server spawn or loot roll order. Grinding Gear Games also documents the use of multiple simultaneous sources of variation in its own design presentations. [ExileCon procedural world generation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EXnoHTqO7TE) · [GDC procedural replayability](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1025784/Designing-Path-of-Exile-to)

Hearth Below can sustain variety by varying route topology, room footprint, scenery, and population independently. Give each reachable branch a purpose and a plausible encounter budget. Anchor selected guarded chests to a purposeful dead end. Check spacing and navigation of every generated encounter independently from the number of room and prop records. Test batches by actual placed footprints and population, and spot-check Forward+ runs where approach angles and warning sightlines matter.

## Skyrim terrain and material layering

A `TESObjectLAND` loader and a related routine at `0x1402A9170` and `0x1402AD5C0` branch on the native height, vertex-normal, vertex-color, base texture, additional texture, and vertex paint tags. The `BGSTextureSet` loader at `0x14032D1D0` separately reads texture paths. Record scans counted 15,564 `LAND` records, including 9,598 with base textures and 9,389 with additional texture and vertex paint records. First sampled cells already combine field grass, snow, soil, and rock references across their quadrants. Linked base-game BSA v105 entries confirm separate DDS maps for road, tree bark, and other materials. See the earlier [material reference study](../../MODEL_CATALOGUE.md#material-reference-study) for observations from 120 locally reviewed albedos.

For original Hearth Below materials, use independent tileable layers and vary the blend at landscape scale. Treat meadow, worn road, shale, and vegetation as separate materials with aligned texture scale. Use the terrain's slope, height, or local paint to shape transitions. Choose muted mineral variation and plausible wear, with chips and seams at the object's scale. Keep authored lighting out of albedo channels where the engine supplies it.

## Skyrim clock, climate, and regional weather

The installed base `Skyrim.esm` has separate `GLOB` values for calendar time and time scale. The defaults are `GameHour = 8` and `TimeScale = 20`, corresponding to a 72-minute real-time day at the default rate. These are base data defaults. Tamriel links to `SkyrimClimate`, whose first four `TNAM` bytes are `33, 60, 96, 123` in ten-minute units. They delimit dawn at 05:30–10:00 and dusk at 16:00–20:30. The same record gives its cloudy weather a weight of 100. The installed master contains six climate records, 84 weather profiles, and 53 regions with weather lists.

Regional records supply local alternatives. `WeatherTundraMarsh` has weights 45 cloudy, 25 clear, 20 fog, and 10 overcast rain after grouping the cloudy and clear variants. `WeatherCoastFog` specifies one fog profile at weight 100. `WTHR` records store separate fog, multiple time-of-day colors, ambient and imagespace data, and optional sound references. Rain presets carry different fog and sound data from the base clear preset. The installed Creation Kit archive `Data/Scripts.zip` includes `Weather.psc` and `Utility.psc`. Their APIs distinguish the active and outgoing weather, transition fraction, weather override, and game time in elapsed game days.

A read-only Ghidra replay on the verified executable decompiled the climate loader at `0x1402A59F0`, the weather-list loader at `0x14030A5D0`, the weather loader at `0x140308290`, and regional weather data handling at `0x140253D20`. They confirm separate timing bytes, weather entries with per-entry weights and optional global references, weather presentation fields, and regional override and priority handling. Runtime registration at `0x140A63E30` links the shipped weather API to separate active-weather, outgoing-weather, and transition-fraction fields. The scripted type lookup at `0x140A65110` checks regional or climate lists according to context. The automatic weather scheduler, transition easing curve, and effective settings of individual saves or mod load orders remain unverified.

For Hearth Below, keep the world clock independent of weather selection. Author several lighting and fog states across the clock, blend gradual changes from the outgoing weather, and increase local mist at the marsh boundary so the whole valley need not share the same visibility. Verify that day and night each read outdoors, that a rain transition changes both sky and ground-level presentation, and that crossing into and out of the marsh gives a continuous change. The local replay uses `ReferenceCodeBodies.java` with `-process SkyrimSE.exe -readOnly -noanalysis` on the retained project. The record query and decompile logs are in `%TEMP%/hearth-greywake-skyrim-climate`.

# Creature surface textures

ImageGen produced these original albedo textures for the seven Blender creature models. The packed Blender sources are under `assets/source/creatures`.

## `creature_skins.png`

Create one game albedo texture atlas, exact 2 by 2 edge-to-edge quadrants, no title, no lighting, no text, no full animals. This is an original realistic dark medieval fantasy surface map to be wrapped onto creature meshes. Top left is smoothly weathered black wolf fur, directional short uneven strands running top to bottom, charcoal base with muted dark neutral warm brown brindling readable in low light; top right is dusty grey-brown segmented spider exoskeleton texture, desaturated pyrite and soot with fine meandering hairline cracks and small pits, NO rendered glints; bottom left is weathered ivory chitin and dry bone with faint yellowed cracks, slight warm grey-brown embedded dust, fine small striations; bottom right is weathered dried dark red/brown skin for a giant beast with muted slight wrinkling and mottling, aged dry surface grading. Scans in diffuse neutral light or tasteful hand-painted material, the same completely flat plane, no deep black shadows or baked specular, no labels, no border or space between quadrants. Balance textures to stay legible in dim torchlight and avoid wax or plastic. Exact squares.

## `burial_wing.png`

Create a single seamless-in-use full-bleed square game albedo material painted from real moth scales for the front of the wing of an original giant dark medieval fantasy hawkmoth. The wing geometry will be cut out in the game, the entire canvas must have texture, no blank background or visible silhouette. Directional wing root is middle-left, ribs and scale rows fan outward to the upper-right and lower-right. Muted pale ochre taupe, grey-brown charcoal, desaturated cream broad irregular bands with fine smoky radial pattern. One softly irregular natural ring eyespot, dark muted red ochre with thin faded buff edge, center at 74 percent from left and 45 percent from top, small subtle dotted dust along ribs. Fine natural lepidopteran scales, analog hand-painted reference-game material in diffuse flat unlit albedo, no object shadows, no high-gloss, no graphic vector pattern. No whole wings, veins in high relief, backgrounds, logos, typography, or separately lit objects. Flat surface map only.
